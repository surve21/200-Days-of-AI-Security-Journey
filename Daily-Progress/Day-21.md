##  Why Cryptography Matters

Cryptography is an important foundation of cybersecurity because it provides mechanisms for protecting information against unauthorized access and manipulation.

It supports major security objectives such as:

* **Confidentiality** — protecting information from unauthorized disclosure.
* **Integrity** — detecting unauthorized modification of information.
* **Authentication** — helping verify identities and the authenticity of information.
* **Non-repudiation** — providing evidence that an action or message originated from a particular party.
