##  Security Perspective

Today's learning focused on the importance of protecting the **identity and access layer** of an organization.

Understanding account security is important for defensive cybersecurity because compromised credentials are commonly involved in unauthorized access and account takeover scenarios.

---

##  Key Takeaways

- Cybersecurity involves protecting systems, networks, applications, accounts, and data.
- Strong authentication is an important defensive security control.
- Password reuse increases the impact of credential compromise.
- MFA provides an additional layer of protection beyond passwords.
- Security requires both technical controls and secure user practices.
