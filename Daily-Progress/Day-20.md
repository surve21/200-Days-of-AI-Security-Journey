##  Phase 1 — Security Mindset

### Module 1: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

**Key Terms Covered**

* **Asset** — Something valuable that needs protection.
* **Threat** — A potential cause of harm to an asset.
* **Vulnerability** — A weakness that can be exploited.
* **Risk** — The potential impact and likelihood of a threat exploiting a vulnerability.
* **Exploit** — A technique, code, or method used to take advantage of a vulnerability.

**Key Learning**

Understanding these terms is essential for developing a security mindset and analyzing how attacks can affect an organization's assets.

---

### Module 2: CIA Triad

Studied the three fundamental objectives of information security:

* **Confidentiality** — Preventing unauthorized access to information.
* **Integrity** — Preventing unauthorized modification or destruction of information.
* **Availability** — Ensuring systems and information are accessible when needed.

**Key Learning**

The CIA Triad provides a fundamental framework for evaluating security requirements and understanding the objectives of security controls.

---

### Module 3: AAA

Studied the three components of AAA security:

* **Authentication** — Verifying who a user or system is.
* **Authorization** — Determining what an authenticated user or system is allowed to access.
* **Accounting** — Recording and tracking activities and access.

**Key Learning**

AAA helps organizations control access to resources and maintain accountability for user and system activities.

---

### Module 4: Security Controls and Core Principles

**Topics Covered**

* Security Controls
* Preventive Controls
* Detective Controls
* Corrective Controls
* Administrative Controls
* Technical Controls
* Physical Controls
* Core Security Principles

**Key Learning**

Security controls are safeguards designed to reduce risk and protect organizational assets. Effective security requires multiple layers of controls rather than relying on a single security mechanism.

---

##  Security Mindset

Today's learning helped me understand that cybersecurity is not only about using security tools.

A security mindset involves continuously asking:

> **What are we protecting?**
> **What could go wrong?**
> **What weaknesses exist?**
> **What is the impact?**
> **What controls can reduce the risk?**

This mindset will be important as I progress toward defensive security, SOC operations, and AI Security Engineering.

---

##  Key Takeaways

* Assets are what we need to protect.
* Threats can take advantage of vulnerabilities.
* Risk helps us understand the potential impact of security threats.
* The CIA Triad provides the foundation for information security.
* AAA controls identity, access, and accountability.
* Security controls reduce risk through preventive, detective, and corrective measures.
* A strong security mindset focuses on identifying and reducing risk rather than simply reacting to attacks.
